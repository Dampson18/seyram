Make comfort last

A little care goes a long way.

Use these general care tips to keep your home essentials feeling fresh. Always follow the care label supplied with your specific item.

Bedding

Keep sheets soft and fresh

  • Check the item label before washing for fabric-specific instructions.
  • Wash similar colours together and use a suitable, mild detergent.
  • Avoid overloading the machine; dry fully before storing or making the bed.

Throws

Protect a fluffy finish

  • Check the care label first; pile and fabric can need different wash settings.
  • Use a gentle cycle only when the label allows, and avoid high heat.
  • Air the throw out between uses and store it completely dry.

Cushions

Refresh without losing shape

  • Separate a removable cover from its insert before cleaning when possible.
  • Spot-test any cleaner on a discreet area first.
  • Fluff and reshape the cushion after use; let cleaned parts dry fully.

Pillows & bolsters

Give support items a reset

  • Follow the supplied label and do not wash an insert unless care instructions allow it.
  • Air items regularly in a clean, shaded, well-ventilated place.
  • Use a removable cover where available to make routine cleaning easier.

Care labels take priority. These are general suggestions, not a substitute for the instructions on your item. Contact us if you need help checking a product's care details.
