About SÉYRA

Home should feel as good as it looks.

Our story

Style and comfort for everyday living.

SÉYRA brings dresses and home essentials together in one place. From finding a style for your wardrobe to choosing bedsheets and cushions for your space, we make it easy to ask questions and check what is available.

Every order is handled personally, by call or WhatsApp, so you can confirm the current styles, sizes, prices and delivery details before you pay.

What guides us

A few things we don't compromise on.

Fabric quality

We choose materials for how they feel and how long they last — not just how they photograph.

Fit for every bed

Bedsheets come in all sizes, because a beautiful set that doesn't fit isn't much use.

Honest pricing

Clear prices from the start, with no pressure to buy more than you came for.

Reach across Ghana

Wherever you're ordering from, we'll work out an affordable way to get it to you.

Let's get your home sorted.

Browse what's available or reach out directly — we're happy to help you choose.
