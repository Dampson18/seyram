Good to know

Clear expectations, before checkout.

  • Fees vary by destination. There is no flat nationwide fee; we quote the delivery cost for your location.
  • Timing depends on the route. We provide an estimate when your order is confirmed rather than promising a fixed arrival date.
  • Please check your order at handoff. If an item is missing or arrives damaged, contact us promptly with your order details and a photo.
  • Need to change or cancel? Message us as soon as possible. Changes depend on whether the order has already been prepared or dispatched.

Not sure if we reach your area?

Send us your location and the items you want. We will check delivery options and confirm costs before you commit.
